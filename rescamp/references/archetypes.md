# Research archetypes and neutral mappings

Every campaign uses the universal architecture. Activate only the archetype-specific checks needed by the project. Mixed projects may combine several.

## Experimental

Clarify construct/system, intervention, controls, measurement validity, replication, randomization/blinding where appropriate, sample-size rationale, safety, material handling, and preregistered versus exploratory analyses.

## Computational or modeling

Clarify datasets, leakage boundaries, baselines, environments, seeds, software/model versions, hardware, metrics, ablations, held-out evaluation, reproducibility, and compute budget. Treat generated code and model judgments as fallible instruments.

## Observational or quantitative

Clarify target population, estimand, sampling frame, measurement, confounding, missingness, temporal ordering, identification assumptions, sensitivity analyses, multiplicity, and generalizability. Do not imply causality without a defensible design.

## Qualitative, field, or ethnographic

Clarify research setting, participants or materials, access and positionality, sampling logic, consent, recording and storage, coding/interpretation protocol, negative cases, reflexivity, saturation or stopping rationale, cultural safety, and member/stakeholder checks when appropriate.

## Humanities or interpretive

Clarify corpus/canon boundaries, periodization, languages/editions, provenance, archival access, source criticism, interpretive framework, rival readings, negative evidence, anachronism risk, translation policy, quotation/copyright rules, and standards for warranted claims. Numerical scoring and experimental controls are optional, not defaults.

## Conceptual, philosophical, legal, or normative

Clarify the question type, definitions, premises, jurisdiction or tradition, argument structure, counterexamples, objections, precedent/source hierarchy, normative principles, scope conditions, and adjudication rule. Distinguish descriptive evidence from normative conclusions.

## Evidence synthesis or literature review

Clarify review question, source types, databases/corpora, date/language limits, inclusion/exclusion, search auditability, screening, quality appraisal, extraction, synthesis method, heterogeneity, publication bias, update date, and living-review policy.

## Policy or program evaluation

Clarify decision owner, theory of change, stakeholders, implementation context, comparators, outcomes and unintended effects, equity/distributional effects, causal or contribution logic, data rights, political constraints, feasibility, and decision thresholds.

## Design or engineering

Clarify users and use conditions, requirements, interfaces, constraints, hazards, alternatives, verification versus validation, prototypes, failure modes, tolerance, lifecycle, maintainability, standards, and acceptance tests.

## Creative practice or practice-based research

Clarify research contribution, practice/process records, works or artifacts, audience/context, critical framework, reflective method, evaluation or critique process, rights, and how practice evidence supports the stated inquiry.

## Mixed methods

State why each method is needed, sequence or concurrency, integration point, conflict-resolution rule, relative priority, sampling relationship, and how combined evidence changes the conclusion.

## Universal non-STEM safeguards

- Do not invent a quantitative metric where reasoned adjudication is the correct instrument.
- Do not require falsifiability for a purely interpretive or normative claim; require explicit counterarguments, contrary evidence, or scope conditions instead.
- Do not treat source volume as source quality.
- Preserve ambiguity when the evidence cannot adjudicate it.
- Record researcher positionality or normative commitments when they can affect interpretation.
